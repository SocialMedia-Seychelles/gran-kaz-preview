What people host

Small or large, relaxed or dressed up. Tell us the occasion and we will shape the night around it.

  • After-work drinks

    The team, a few rounds and somewhere to sit that isn't the office.

  • Birthdays

    Cocktails, a table that keeps filling up and room to dance.

  • Company nights

    Launches, send-offs and year-end parties, with packages to match.

  • Private dinners

    The grill, the bar and the Patio, all to yourselves.

How it works

  1. Tell us the plan

    The date, how many of you and what you are celebrating.

  2. Pick a package

    We suggest food and drinks packages that fit your numbers and budget.

  3. Turn up

    We set up the Patio and look after the rest.
