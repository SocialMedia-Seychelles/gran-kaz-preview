Gran Kaz Supports Seychelles Sailing Ahead of Star Sailors League Gold Cup in Rio

Gran Kaz is proud to support Seychelles Sailing Team as they prepare to compete in the Star Sailors League (SSL) Gold Cup in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, this November.

As part of its commitment to supporting local sports and promoting Seychellois talent on the international stage, Gran Kaz has sponsored team uniforms and provided financial assistance to the sum of SCR15,000 to help the sailors prepare for the upcoming competition.

The support aims to contribute towards the team’s preparations as they get ready to represent Seychelles against international competitors at one of the sport’s major sailing events.

Gran Kaz remains committed to supporting initiatives that create opportunities for local athletes, encourage sporting development and allow Seychellois talent to proudly represent the country beyond its shores.
