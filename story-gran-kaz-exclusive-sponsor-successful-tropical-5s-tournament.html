Gran Kaz Exclusive Sponsor of the Successful Tropical 5’s Tournament

The golden sands of Beau Vallon Beach came alive on Saturday, 20th September, as cheers, music, and the crash of waves set the stage for an unforgettable day of rugby. Eight teams battled it out in this year’s edition of the Tropical 5’s Beach Rugby Tournament, with fans gathering along the shoreline to witness fast-paced matches, fierce rivalries, and plenty of community spirit.

Among the competitors were some familiar faces. Local favourites — the Shearwaters, the Pirates, the Sharks, the Seychelles Men’s team, the Marlins, and the Black Parrots — returned for the fourth year running, each determined to seize the coveted trophy. Joining them were two international teams: Dubai’s defending champions, Barrelhouse, back to protect their title, and newcomers from Moscow State University (MSU), Russia, who brought fresh energy and international flair to the competition.

The day was filled with drama and excitement, culminating in a nail-biting final between the Marlins and MSU. With both teams giving everything they had, it was the Marlins who triumphed, securing victory with a tight 3-2 scoreline and lifting the trophy to thunderous applause from the home crowd.

As the exclusive sponsor of the 2025 tournament, Gran Kaz played a pivotal role in making the event not just about sport, but about community celebration. With a SCR 50,000 sponsorship, Gran Kaz not only ensured the smooth running of the tournament but also elevated the atmosphere with music, food, and entertainment throughout the day — staying true to its role as a leader in entertainment.

This year’s edition also marked the beginning of an exciting grassroots initiative, with the tournament organizers engaging schoolchildren to introduce rugby at a youth level. For Gran Kaz, this vision of nurturing young talent and promoting healthy, active lifestyles aligns perfectly with the company’s values of empowerment and inclusivity.

From thrilling beachside rugby action to music, laughter, and the spirit of togetherness, the 2025 Tropical 5’s was a resounding success.
