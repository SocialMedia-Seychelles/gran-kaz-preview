Gran Kaz Supports Basketball Development in Seychelles with SCR 10,000 Sponsorship

Gran Kaz has reaffirmed its commitment to sports development in Seychelles with a sponsorship of SCR 10,000 to the Seychelles Basketball Federation (SBF) 3x3 Commission, in support of its upcoming FIBA Coach and Player National Camp.

The official handover took place on Thursday, 29 October 2026, at Kenwyn House, ahead of the facilitator's arrival in Seychelles.

The national camp is an important development initiative that brings together local coaches and players for structured technical training, coaching education and high-level development sessions. It aims to strengthen coaching standards, improve player performance and contribute to the long-term growth of basketball in Seychelles.

Beyond developing local talent, the programme will also contribute to preparations for Seychelles' national basketball teams ahead of the upcoming Africa Cup in Madagascar, providing valuable opportunities to strengthen their skills and competitiveness on the continental stage.

For Gran Kaz, supporting sports remains a key pillar of its community engagement efforts. The benefits of sports extend far beyond the playing field, promoting physical and mental well-being while instilling important values such as discipline, teamwork, commitment and resilience. Sporting initiatives also provide meaningful opportunities for personal growth, encourage positive youth engagement and help build stronger, more connected communities.

Through partnerships such as this, Gran Kaz continues to recognise the importance of investing in athletes, coaches and sporting programmes that create opportunities for individuals to develop and contribute to the wider community.

Gran Kaz remains committed to supporting initiatives that make a positive difference in Seychelles, with a continued focus on community development, youth empowerment and building opportunities for future generations.
