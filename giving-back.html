Echoes of Time: heritage through the lens

The walls of Kenwyn House came alive on Tuesday evening, August 19, as history, memory, and artistry converged in the third edition of the Kenwyn House Amateur Photography Competition.

Kenwyn House

Built in 1855 and declared a National Monument in 1984, Kenwyn House stood in the heart of Victoria for generations before the years caught up with it. In 2018 we took on its lease and, with its owners Cable and Wireless Seychelles, restored it.

Today it is a free public art gallery where Seychellois artists show their work, and home to the first Coco de Mer Wall in Seychelles, set up with the Seychelles Islands Foundation.
